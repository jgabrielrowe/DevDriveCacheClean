# Full Disk Access

DDCC can scan without Full Disk Access. If macOS blocks a folder, DDCC marks
affected sizes with `+` or shows a message that some folders could not be read.

The banner at the top of the window means DDCC does not currently have Full
Disk Access. Choose **Open Settings**, add DDCC under Privacy & Security > Full
Disk Access, then quit and reopen DDCC if the banner remains.

Full Disk Access mainly improves completeness. DDCC targets developer caches
and user-owned files that are usually readable without it. Protected system and
app data may still be unavailable or shown only for information.

Grant Full Disk Access if you want the most complete scan DDCC can provide.
Leave it off if you only want DDCC to inspect files available to ordinary apps.
